v0.5.4 — Security release

• Biometric unlock is bound to the hardware
  vault: the calendar opens only if the device
  really authenticated your fingerprint, not on
  the app's word alone.
• If the device's biometrics change, biometric
  unlock switches off and tells you.
• A huge imported calendar can no longer freeze
  the display.
• .ics import: a malformed repeat rule no
  longer costs the event its recurrence.
