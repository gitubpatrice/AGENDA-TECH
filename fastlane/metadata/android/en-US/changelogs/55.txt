v1.0.3 — A binary with no stowaway

• No change to the app itself: this release
  carries no functional change.
• Google's build tooling was quietly placing
  an encrypted list of our libraries inside
  the APK, meant for its Play console. We do
  not publish on Play, and an unreadable blob
  has no place in a binary whose whole point
  is being verifiable. It is now gone.
