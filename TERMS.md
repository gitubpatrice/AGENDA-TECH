## Data loss

Your agenda exists **only on your phone**, encrypted. That is the point of the app, and it has
consequences no one can undo for you:

- **A forgotten backup password makes that backup unreadable forever.** For a backup you export by
  hand, the password is stored nowhere; it only derives the encryption key. No recovery procedure
  exists, including on our side.
- **Restoring a backup replaces** the current calendars, events and reminders. This cannot be
  undone.
- **A forgotten app PIN cannot be reset.** Without biometrics, the only way back into the app is to
  clear its data from Android's settings, which erases the agenda. Keep a backup.
- **Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes the agenda.** So can a factory reset, or a
  change of the device's secure hardware that makes the encryption key unreachable.

An encrypted backup, kept off the phone, is the only protection against all of these. The app offers
to make one, and can make one every week on its own.
